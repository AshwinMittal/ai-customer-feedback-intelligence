# Portfolio project interview story (complete after project)

**Honest attribution:** Independent learning project with synthetic data. Not production AI shipped in employment.

- **Problem:** Why does analysis of customer feedback matter? For whom?
- **My role:** What decisions did I personally make versus ask a model to produce?
- **Solution:** LLM-assisted classification, citation/evidence check, PM synthesis.
- **Design trade-offs:** Controlled taxonomy vs open themes, manual review vs full automation, cost vs quality.
- **Evaluation:** Number of examples, label accuracy, evidence checks, human rubric, prompt versions.
- **Failures:** At least two concrete mistakes the system made and how they changed the design.
- **Outcome:** Report measured outcomes from your runs; don't invent production time savings or adoption.
- **Next steps:** More diverse data, multi-label, confidence calibration, RAG where relevant, continuous evals.
